A junior developer releases a package to the world because they think they learned something new. In reality, they didn't. Somebody already built it, and probably built it better. And that's fine. That's good, even. That's how you learn.

<!--more-->

You reinvent the wheel a few times. You build the thing that already exists, you ship it, and somewhere in the middle of making it you find out why the existing one is shaped the way it is. Then, suddenly, you know something. The package was never the point. The learning was.

But something has changed. With AI, people are going so fast that they skip the learning part entirely. They still make the shit product. They just push it out the door without ever sitting in the making of it long enough to learn anything from it.

The shit product used to be tuition. Now it's just output.

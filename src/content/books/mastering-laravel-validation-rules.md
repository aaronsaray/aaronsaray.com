Let's upgrade your app's security, improve your data quality, and stop bad input before it becomes a bug. 
That's what the right validation provides.  The docs tell you which rules exist, but it can be overwhelming with so 
many choices. And what happens when you choose the wrong one? This book walks through common scenarios and gives you
practical knowledge.  It's an easier read than you're imagining, and by the end, you'll stop guessing and be
a master at Laravel validation.
